Our Story

More than food — we make moments.

Sila's Kitchen and More was born out of a deep love for community, celebration, and food that truly brings people together. Since 2024, we have been crafting handmade Nigerian small chops, pastries, refreshing drinks, and yoghurt treats with pride.

From intimate family gatherings to large corporate events, every order is prepared with freshness and passion, from small chops and pastries to drinks and yoghurt treats. No pre-made stock — everything is prepared fresh specifically for your date.

We are proudly homegrown, built on the principle that Quality Service Is Our Benchmark.
