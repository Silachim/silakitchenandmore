Payment & Receipt Workflow

When a customer submits payment, the payment should appear in this operational queue. Sila can inspect the payment, review the receipt, verify or reject the payment, and record an administrative note.

Verification is performed through the existing verify_payment database function, while rejection uses reject_payment.
